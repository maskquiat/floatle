Note for the teacher

Sub reports

Notes your substitutes left from a shared board. Only you can see them. Reports are deleted for good after 30 days.

Schedule boards

Give each class board a start time. When the bell time comes, Floatle switches to that board on its own, in Present mode and the Float window too. It works while Floatle is open in a browser tab.

Screen crawler

A strip along the bottom of the screen, like a news ticker. It shows the notes you pick from this board and keeps up as you edit them.

Running late?

Let class start without you. Whoever is covering opens your board with the code in your sub binder and sees a start-of-class screen: the first activity, a timer, and a few steps for them.

Questions, problems and ideas

Something not working, a question, or an idea for Floatle? Send it here. Every note is read, and it helps decide what gets fixed and built next.

Background

Sub lesson plan

A one-page plan made from this board's notes. Add what the notes don't say, then print it or save it as a PDF. Everything here saves as you type.

Today's lesson plan

If this screen is on the projector, students can see this panel. Print it, or open it in a new window on your own screen.

Saved lessons

Name and save everything on a board: notes, timers, documents, the background and your sub plans. Open it again on any board, any day. If the board is shared with a sub, they see the lesson right away.
