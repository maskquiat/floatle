Refund Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

If Floatle Pro isn't right for you, you can get a full refund within 30 days of any payment. No conditions, and no reason needed.

How it works

  • The 30 days count from the date of each payment. That includes your first payment and every renewal, monthly or yearly.
  • The refund is the full amount you paid for that payment.
  • After a refund, your account moves to the free plan. Your boards are never deleted; boards beyond the free limit become view-only.

How to ask for a refund

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns.

You can ask for a refund either way:

  • Use the link in your Paddle receipt email, or visit paddle.net.
  • Email billing@floatle.com with the email address on your account, and we'll take care of it with Paddle.

Refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank may take a few days to show it.

Canceling

You can cancel Pro anytime to stop future charges. If you cancel after 30 days, Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

This policy is in addition to any refund rights you have under the law and Paddle's Refund Policy.
