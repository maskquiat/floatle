Questions

Do students need accounts?

No. Teachers hold the accounts. Students only see a board when a teacher or substitute displays it.

What happens to my boards if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. Boards beyond the free 2 become view-only until you upgrade again or choose which 2 stay active.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. You can get a full refund within 30 days of any payment, no questions asked. See the Refund Policy.

Who handles payments?

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns.
