Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Floatle is run by Kenneth Muigai, doing business as Floatle. The short version: we collect only what we need to run Floatle, we never sell your information, and you can delete your data at any time.

1. Who Floatle is for

Floatle accounts are for teachers and other adults. Students do not create accounts, and Floatle is not directed to children under 13. Students may see a board when a teacher or substitute displays it in class.

2. What we collect

WhatExamplesWhy
Account detailsYour email address, and your name if you add oneTo sign you in and contact you about your account
What you put on boardsNotes, agendas, timers, images, attached documents, board namesTo save and sync your boards and show them where you choose
Shared-board activityBoard codes, sub reports, drawings a substitute addsSo substitutes can open your board and report back to you
Payment detailsPlan, billing country, receiptsCollected by Paddle. We never see or store your full card number.
Usage informationPages visited, features used, device and browser typeTo fix problems and decide what to improve
MessagesEmails or suggestions you send usTo answer you

Sub reports are written for the teacher and should use students' first names or initials only. Please don't put sensitive student records on Floatle, such as health details, IEP or 504 documents, or grades.

3. How we use it

  • To run Floatle: saving boards, syncing devices, sharing boards with substitutes
  • To handle Pro subscriptions through Paddle
  • To answer support questions and send important account notices
  • To understand which features are used, so we can improve them
  • To keep Floatle secure and prevent misuse

We only send product news or tips if you opt in, and every such email has an unsubscribe link.

4. What we never do

  • We never sell your information or student information.
  • We never use board content for advertising.
  • We never share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Services that help run Floatle

We use a small number of trusted providers. Each one only receives what it needs to do its job.

For analytics, we don't send the text of your notes, document names or sub reports, and on-screen text is masked. Shared views opened by substitutes are counted without identifying anyone.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of Floatle's users.

6. Schools and districts

If your school or district needs a data privacy agreement before staff use Floatle (for example, under New York Education Law § 2-d), contact privacy@floatle.com and we will work with you on it.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your boards and files stay as long as your account is open.
  • Deleted boards go to Trash for 30 days, then are removed.
  • If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your boards, files and account details within 30 days. Payment records are kept by Paddle as the law requires.

8. Your choices

  • See or export your data: use the export feature in Floatle, or email privacy@floatle.com.
  • Correct it: update your details in Floatle, or ask us to.
  • Delete it: email privacy@floatle.com from the email on your account.
  • Stop optional emails: use the unsubscribe link in any of them.

We will answer requests within 30 days.

9. Keeping data safe

Floatle uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), and accounts are protected by sign-in through our database provider. Board codes are meant for teachers and substitutes only, so keep them in your sub plans rather than posting them where students can see them. No online service is perfectly secure, but if we learn of a breach affecting your information, we will tell you promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make an important change, we will update this page and email account holders before it takes effect.
